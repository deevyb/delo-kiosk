## Changelog

- **Aug 23** — Tap to Pay with Venmo shipped, behind `/order?pay=1`. After Send, the confirmation becomes a customer-facing payment screen the order-taker flips around: name, drink, flat price at arm's-length size, a QR fallback, and an animated "tap your phone under here" cue at the bottom — bottom because that's where the NFC sticker physically sits on the back of their phone, and phones read with their top edge. The sticker and the QR carry the same `venmo.com` universal link, so the customer's phone opens Venmo with handle, amount, and note prefilled. Handle and price are env-only (`NEXT_PUBLIC_VENMO_HANDLE` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_VENMO_PRICE`) — no prices anywhere near the menu or schema — and with either unset the mode hides itself from `/` and the NavMenu while `?pay=1` falls back to the ordinary screen. Plain `/order` is untouched, 3s reset and all, so free events keep exactly today's flow. Owner-facing hardware guide (stickers, writing the tag, placement, per-event test): `docs/tap-to-pay-setup.md`. Worth noting for later: item #7 (queue position on the confirmation) now has a confirmation that stays up until dismissed to compose with, instead of a 3-second timer to fight.
- **Aug 2** — `app/kitchen/page.tsx` now degrades instead of throwing when the database is unreachable: it returns an empty list plus a `loadFailed` flag, the client seeds the banner from it, and the mount sync fills the display the moment the database answers. One outage screen instead of two — previously which one a barista saw depended on whether the failure started before or after the page mounted, a distinction that means nothing to the person holding the iPad. `error.tsx` survives for genuine crashes only, with cause-neutral copy. Verified by pointing Supabase at a closed port: the display comes up working, banner already up, and recovers on restart. Also found while testing — Retry gave _no_ feedback on a fast failure, because React batches the two setStates into one render when the promise settles in a microtask, so the label never painted; it now holds for a minimum visible moment.
- **Aug 2** — Connection banner rewritten into three honest states (`live` / `delayed` / `unreachable`) with a Retry button, because "Offline" on every realtime drop is now misleading — the sync keeps delivering. Added `app/kitchen/error.tsx`: `page.tsx` throws when the database is unreachable and a client ErrorBoundary can't catch a Server Component failure, so that case used to show a raw Next.js error page. Review passes caught that Next's `reset()` can't recover a server-render failure (it only clears boundary state), and that Retry silently no-opped while a sync was in flight — both fixed.
- **Aug 2** — Kitchen display re-syncs itself every 30s so a realtime outage can't hide orders, with an immediate catch-up on reconnect and on wake. Verified by killing the WebSocket and blocking reconnects: an order inserted straight into the database appeared in 11.3s. A triple-lens review (race / hostile-network / React-lifecycle) all independently found the same defect — overlapping syncs could revert a finished drink back into the queue, via a path the write-generation guard structurally can't see. Also fixed: realtime and PostgREST encode `timestamptz` differently, which was rebuilding every card each sync _and_ masking a collision that could drop a claim permanently; the sync window was measured from the iPad's clock, so a badly-set device fetched nothing while looking healthy.
